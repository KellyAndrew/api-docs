Welcome to **KyaniteAPI**. Kyanite is a high-conductivity event mesh designed to ensure structural integrity between your frontend actions and backend logic.

### The Big Picture

Kyanite acts as the **bedrock** for your application data. Instead of hitting your primary database directly, clients transmit signals to our **Ingress Stratum**. This ensures that even during seismic traffic spikes, your data is captured and crystallized for processing without fracturing the user experience.

### Core Features

* **Crystalline Validation:** Every payload is checked against your defined JSON schemas instantly to ensure data purity before it enters the mesh.
* **Non-Blocking Conduction:** Immediate `202 Accepted` responses keep your client interactions fluid and fast.
* **Solid-State Reliability:** Integrated message queuing ensures no signal is ever lost, even during downstream maintenance cycles.

### Architecture Strata

| Component | Layer | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Surface Node** | Security | Handles Auth (Crystal-Key) and initial request validation. |
| **The Conduit** | Transport | The high-speed, anisotropic lane for your data packets. |
| **Refractor Pool** | Logic | Distributed services that transform and route data based on priority. |
| **Bedrock Storage** | Persistence | The final destination (Database, Analytics, or S3). |
